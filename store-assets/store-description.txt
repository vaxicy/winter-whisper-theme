Winter Whisper Theme brings a quiet winter moment to Chrome. A muted lavender frame wraps the top of the window, the toolbar and active tab cool into soft periwinkle, and the new tab page opens on icy blue. Deep gray-violet text keeps tab titles, bookmarks, the address bar and toolbar icons clear, while deep mist lavender marks the new tab header and links. Flat solid colors throughout — no wallpaper, no textures, no gradients, no permissions.
